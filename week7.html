Quick Summary

3D Printing Methods

  • FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling)
    Uses melted plastic filament such as PLA, ABS, PETG.
    Prints layer by layer and is suitable for functional parts and prototypes.

  • SLA (Stereolithography)
    Uses UV light to cure liquid resin.
    Produces smooth, high-detail prints for precision applications.
